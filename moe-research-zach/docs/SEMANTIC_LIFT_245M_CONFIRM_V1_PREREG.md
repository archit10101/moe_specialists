## 1. Question and status

**Question.** Does Semantic Lift-Weighted Expert Learning (sll; `src/csr/sll_lift.py`) produce greater *real
subject-specific functional specialization* than:

1. its shuffled-group control (primary);
2. ordinary Semantic C6;
3. explicit-label EDL;
4. TOKEN-4?

**This is a confirmation, not a search.**
- The mechanism is frozen at the configuration tested at 50M in sll v1.
- Nothing is tuned after results are seen.
- No intermediate result can modify the experiment.

**Prior evidence, not mixed into this test.** sll v1 seeds 100 and 101 at 50M:
- The held-out gate was rejected (diagnosis C).
- The post-terminal descriptive VAL pass showed a positive real-domain E−F interaction in both seeds, with the CI excluding 0 in one.

## 2. Frozen method (nothing below may change)

**Backbone (Semantic C6).**
- 103,609,728-parameter LFR: 12 layers (11 MoE), d 384, 32 routed experts with FFN 256, shared expert with FFN 768, unrestricted dropless top-4 routing, gates = softmax probabilities.
- FINAL plan: 3,880 steps, 245,032,457 tokens, plan sha256 `f4bc3c93…`.
- 64 × 1024 rows per step.
- AdamW (0.9, 0.95), lr 6e-4 WSD (1% warm-up, last 20% decay to 6e-5), weight decay 0.1, clip 1.0, Switch 0.01, z 0.001, bf16 autocast.
- Init `LFRModel(cfg, seed)`; token order fixed per plan.
- Semantic-EDL `paper_latent` β 0.005 on all 11 MoE layers, using the frozen K16 table `table_SEM_T05.npz` (sha `5d23129b…`).
- Shuffled control: the persistent paper permutation `table_SHUF_SEM_T05.npz` (sha `d374a4ec…`).

**Lift.** `cond["lift"] = {lam 0.5, lo 0.5, hi 2.0, rho 0.98, n0 8, floor 0.02, frac 0.05}`.
- 5% collection (m ≡ 1, native path): steps [0, 194).
- 5% linear ramp: steps [194, 388).
- 90% full strength.
- Slow segment-level lift estimator (EMA ρ 0.98, shrinkage n0 8).
- Token lift s = Σ_k q_k L[l,k,e]; z = s / max(0.02, robust scale); r = exp(λ tanh z).
- m = exact projection of r onto [0.5, 2] with gate-weighted mean 1.
- Only the selected experts' parameter gradients are row-scaled by m (`ScaledExpertMLP`). Router, shared expert, attention, expert-input and all other gradients are unchanged; the forward pass is unchanged.
- All 11 MoE layers. No new loss or mechanism.

## 5. Evaluation (frozen; `slc_eval.py`, `src/csr/slc_lib.py`)

After all eight runs are COMPLETE and their milestones verify, `FINAL_OPEN.json` is written. Then each checkpoint is evaluated once:
- p100 for E, F, C6, SHUF, EDL and T4 × seeds 0–3 (24 checkpoints);
- s50 for E, F, C6 and SHUF × seeds 0–3 (16 checkpoints; trajectory).

The reused T4/EDL fle runs have no s50 checkpoint. All 40 are measured by the identical code.

**Measurement identity (descriptive).** The slc VAL CE of C6_s0 p100 should equal the stored slk value 3.049275 within 1e-4. A mismatch is logged and investigated. All comparisons use slc evaluations only.

### 5.1 Accuracy
- Full VAL CE (token-weighted), CE by real domain, and per-document CE sums and counts.

### 5.2 Routing (VAL audit rows, 200k tokens/domain; routing metrics only, never functional evidence)
- Real-domain routing MI/NMI (layer mean), with load-preserving and document-label-shuffle nulls.
- Domain-conditioned expert-selection distributions (L × 5 × E).
- Mean pairwise top-4 distribution overlap, overall and by layer.
- Domain-to-expert lift profiles P(k|e,l)/P(k).
- Routing-specialised cells: max_k lift ≥ 1.5.
- e5-cluster NMI and cluster-to-expert lift summary.
- Load, normalised routing entropy, under-used experts (full-VAL load < 0.1× uniform) and unused experts.
- Cross-seed consistency of expert subject profiles: per layer, Hungarian matching on profile correlation (`slk_lib.match_profiles`), mean matched correlation over layers and the 6 seed pairs.

### 5.3 Ordinary functional interaction (the existing procedure, unchanged definitions)
- **Discovery:** CALIB-SEARCH paper-half A (200k tokens/domain). k*(l,e) = argmax_k P(k|e,l)/P(k) on calibration tokens only.
- **Measurement:** VAL, 100k tokens/domain (504 documents).
- **Per cell:** one exact equal-compute replacement pass per (l, e). Expert e's router logit at layer l is masked, so the next-ranked expert is admitted and 4 experts stay active. dCE = CE_replace − CE_normal.
- **Interaction:** I(l,e) = mean own-domain dCE − other-domain dCE matched by baseline-CE decile × position bin [0,128), [128,512), [512,1024). This is `slk_lib.interaction_from_stats`.
- **Matching scope.** Layer is fixed within a cell. A document has a single domain, so own and other tokens cannot share a document; documents enter through the document bootstrap.
- **Reported:**
  - the mean over defined cells;
  - per-layer means;
  - a per-cell document bootstrap (1,000 replicates, fixed seed 20261118). Rows are identical for every model, so replicate b is the same document resample for every checkpoint;
  - the number of cells with discovery lift ≥ 1.5 and per-cell CI lower bound > 0;
  - own and generic dCE.

### 5.4 Exposure-conditioned functional interaction (I_cond; same replacement forwards)
- **Tokens:** for cell (l,e), only tokens where e was in the original top-4 at layer l.
- **Strata:**
  - baseline-CE decile (global) × position bin × router rank of e (0–3);
  - × decile of e's gate probability, and × decile of the router margin p_e − p_(5th), where p_(5th) is the probability of the highest non-selected expert, i.e. the replacement candidate. Both deciles are computed within the cell's exposed tokens.
- **Estimator:** exact stratum matching, I_cond = Σ_j w_own(j) (mean own dCE_j − mean other dCE_j) over strata with both own and other exposed tokens, with w_own ∝ the own count.
- **Support:** own exposed / matched tokens, matched fraction, own documents, and the Kish ESS of the other-side weights.
- **A cell is adequate** iff matched own tokens ≥ 100, matched fraction ≥ 0.5, other-side ESS ≥ 50 and own documents ≥ 10.
- No result is claimed for inadequate cells. The model-level I_cond is the mean over adequate cells; the counts are reported.

### 5.5 Safety and pathology rule
A checkpoint is pathological iff:
- the full-VAL load of any expert is < 0.1× uniform or > 4× uniform; or
- the normalised routing entropy is < 0.45; or
- training produced a non-finite loss.

For E/F, multiplier invariants are also checked from the training logs: gate-weighted mean deviation ≤ 1e-6, m within [0.5, 2]. A violation is an integrity failure.

## 6. Statistics

**Per seed s.**
- Δ_s^ord = I_ord(E_s) − I_ord(F_s).
- Δ_s^cond = I_cond(E_s) − I_cond(F_s).
- The same for E−C6, E−EDL and E−T4 (and C6−SHUF, F−SHUF as references).

**Hierarchical bootstrap** (`slc_lib.hier_boot`, 10,000 replicates):
- Each replicate draws 4 seeds with replacement and one document replicate b. The design is crossed (the same documents for every seed), so b is applied to every drawn seed.
- The statistic is the mean over drawn seeds of the per-seed difference under b. The percentile 95% CI is reported.
- **CE** is analysed the same way on full VAL. Document weights are a fixed-seed multinomial resample of the 25,254 VAL documents. The token-weighted CE difference is computed per seed under the weights.
- Document-only intervals are reported per seed but never used for a claim.

**D_func** = (1/4) Σ_s Δ_s^ord, the semantic-versus-shuffled difference. D_func^cond is the same for the conditional interaction.

## 7. Primary claim (E vs F)

The method **supports greater semantic functional specialization** only if all of the following hold:

| criterion | requirement |
|---|---|
| S1 | D_func > 0 |
| S2 | Δ_s^ord > 0 in ≥ 3 of 4 seeds |
| S3 | hierarchical 95% CI of D_func excludes 0 (lower bound > 0) |
| S4 | the exposure-conditioned analysis points the same way: D_func^cond > 0 and Δ_s^cond > 0 in ≥ 3 of 4 seeds |
| S5 | no E or F p100 checkpoint is pathological (5.5), and the multiplier invariants held |

Secondary comparisons (E vs C6, E vs EDL, E vs T4) use the same quantities with hierarchical CIs and seed signs. They are reported without a success claim. Routing NMI, top-4 overlap and specialist-cell counts never substitute for the functional result.

**CE.** Lower is better. The primary accuracy contrasts are E − F, E − C6, E − EDL and E − T4, with per-seed differences, mean ± SD and hierarchical CIs. CE is **worsened** iff mean_s[CE(E_s) − CE(C6_s)] > +0.005 or mean_s[CE(E_s) − CE(EDL_s)] > +0.005. Otherwise it is preserved; 0.005 is the comparability margin used in sll / slk.

## 8. Outcome classification (reported; several may match)

Notation:
- **ORD** = S1 ∧ S2 ∧ S3.
- **COND** = S4.
- **PATH-OK** = S5.

| outcome | definition |
|---|---|
| E | ORD ∧ COND ∧ PATH-OK ∧ CE not worsened: Semantic Lift creates useful subject specialization |
| D | ORD ∧ COND ∧ PATH-OK ∧ CE worsened: specialization with an accuracy trade-off |
| C | ORD ∧ ¬COND: apparent functionality mostly from routing exposure |
| B | ¬ORD ∧ mean_s[I_ord(E) − I_ord(C6)] > 0 with ≥ 3/4 seeds positive ∧ mean_s[I_ord(F) − I_ord(SHUF)] > 0 with ≥ 3/4 seeds positive: group-conditioned gradient allocation helps, semantics do not |
| A | ¬ORD ∧ mean_s[NMI(E) − NMI(F)] > 0 with ≥ 3/4 seeds positive: routing specialization without greater competence |

The primary outcome is the first matching in the order E, D, C, B, A, or "none" if no outcome matches.

**Trajectory** (same quantities at s50 for E/F/C6/SHUF; D50 and D245 are D_func at 50M and 245M):
- **F (transient):** at 50M, mean Δ^ord > 0 with ≥ 3/4 seeds positive, and at 245M not.
- **G (consolidating):** ORD at 245M ∧ D245 > D50. The hierarchical CI of D245 − D50 is reported.
- Otherwise: no trajectory claim (stable or absent).

The method is called **successful** only under outcome E.

## 9. Integrity

**Real-domain labels are evaluation annotations only.** They are never used for training, clustering, router inputs, multipliers, hyperparameters, checkpoint selection or early stopping.

**Prohibited:**
- tuning on validation;
- adding seeds to one condition, or discarding or rerunning seeds;
- changing the metric after results;
- adding tokens or steps, or fine-tuning after 245M;
- modifying baseline results or any pre-existing file;
- claiming significance from document-only intervals.

The locked test split is never read. The protected inventory (8,906 files, `protected_inventory_before.json`, sha `4c88bfe997ef8390…`) is verified at the end.
